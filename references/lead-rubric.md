# ABS Local Prospecting Rubric

Use this reference when qualifying local prospects for Astraeus Business Solutions.

## Best-Fit Businesses

Prioritize owner-operated or small multi-location businesses where the decision-maker is likely reachable:

- Clinics, dentists, physios, chiropractors, wellness, aesthetics, beauty, salons, barbers.
- Gyms, studios, martial arts, coaching, classes, local sports services.
- Trades and home services: electricians, plumbers, builders, landscapers, cleaning, maintenance.
- Restaurants, cafes, takeaways, pubs, hospitality, local accommodation.
- Professional services: accountants, solicitors, consultants, recruiters, estate agents.
- Specialist local shops with consultative sales or booking needs.

Deprioritize large chains, pure franchises, businesses with no clear local decision-maker, obviously closed businesses, or companies already served by a polished agency-grade presence.

## Website Status

- `No site found`: exact-name search finds no credible standalone domain.
- `Social only`: Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, Linktree, marketplace, directory, or booking profile is the main presence.
- `Marketplace/booking only`: relies on Fresha, Treatwell, Deliveroo, Just Eat, Booking.com, Airbnb, or similar without a real owned web presence.
- `Weak site`: standalone website exists but has clear issues.
- `Has site`: credible, modern, mobile-friendly site with clear contact/conversion flow.
- `Unclear`: evidence conflicts or cannot be checked confidently.

## ABS Pain Signals

Website and conversion:

- No clear CTA above the fold.
- Phone or booking path hard to find.
- No mobile-friendly layout.
- Outdated design, broken sections, missing SSL, slow or low-trust feel.
- Thin service pages; no pricing, process, examples, FAQs, testimonials, or local trust markers.
- Strong Google/social activity but weak owned web presence.

Operations and automation:

- Manual booking or enquiry flow only.
- Heavy WhatsApp/DM dependence.
- Repetitive intake questions likely handled manually.
- No obvious CRM or follow-up system.
- Missed review capture or review response workflow.
- Multi-channel leads with no single tracking path.

AI/ops angle:

- Business has knowledge-heavy services, repeated customer questions, eligibility checks, intake forms, quotes, onboarding, or appointment prep.
- Public content suggests owner time is trapped in admin, messages, scheduling, or lead qualification.

Google Business/Profile angle:

- Sparse or inconsistent profile.
- Weak description, missing service categories, low-quality photos, poor review response pattern.
- Inconsistent name/address/phone across sources.

## Offer Angle Templates

Use one specific angle per prospect:

- `Website rebuild to turn social/search traffic into booked enquiries.`
- `Single landing page plus booking/contact flow for local search leads.`
- `Google Business cleanup plus review capture workflow.`
- `Replace DM/WhatsApp-only intake with a simple quote or booking funnel.`
- `CRM-lite follow-up system for missed calls and unconverted enquiries.`
- `AI-assisted FAQ/intake flow to reduce repeated admin questions.`
- `Local SEO and trust-page cleanup for service-area discovery.`

## Outreach Prioritization

Rank first outreach targets by:

1. Clear pain signal.
2. Reachable owner/business contact.
3. Business likely has enough customer value to pay.
4. ABS can credibly ship a small improvement quickly.
5. Evidence quality is at least medium.

Avoid overclaiming ROI. Suggest a concrete improvement, not a vague digital transformation pitch.
